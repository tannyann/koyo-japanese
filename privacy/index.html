Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

Sensei Japan ("we") runs online Japanese lessons and local tours in Japan. This policy explains how we handle the personal information you send us, in line with Japan's Act on the Protection of Personal Information.

1. Information we collect

  • What you send through the forms on this website or by email: your name, email address, and the content of your message (for example your Japanese level, goals, preferred dates and time zone).
  • Payment details when you pay through Stripe: your name, email address and payment status. Your full card number is handled by Stripe and does not reach us.

2. How we use it

  • To reply to your questions and requests
  • To arrange dates and times, and to give lessons or tours
  • To make and send your lesson materials
  • To send payment links and handle payments
  • To keep records needed for our business and for the law

3. Sharing with others

We do not give your personal information to anyone else without your consent, except where the law requires it.

4. Services outside Japan

We use the services below to run the website, email, payments and online lessons. Your information may be stored or processed by these companies in the United States.

  • Formspree (website forms) — United States — privacy policy
  • Google (Gmail, Google Meet) — United States — privacy policy
  • Stripe (payments) — United States — privacy policy
  • Zoom (online lessons) — United States — privacy statement

The United States does not have one comprehensive federal privacy law like Japan's. Personal information there is protected by federal laws for specific sectors and by state laws (such as the California Consumer Privacy Act). Each company describes how it protects data in the policy linked above.

By sending a form or an email to us, you agree that your information is handled by these services as described here.

5. Keeping your information safe

Only the operator of Sensei Japan handles your information. We take care to prevent loss, leaks and unauthorised access.

6. Your requests

You can ask us to tell you what information we hold about you, to correct it, to stop using it, or to delete it. Email us at the address below. There is no fee. On request, we will also tell you the operator's name and address.

7. Contact

senseijapan.jissen@gmail.com

8. Changes

We may update this policy. The latest version is always on this page.

If anything differs between the English and Japanese versions, the Japanese version applies.
